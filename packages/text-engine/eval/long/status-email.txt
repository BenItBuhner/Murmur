Hey team, here is the update for this week on the mobile launch.
The short version is that we are on track for the November twentieth release, but there are two risks I want everyone to know about.

On the engineering side, the new sync engine landed on Tuesday and it has been stable on the beta channel since then.
We saw one crash on older Android phones when the app was restored from the background, and Priya has a fix in review.
Offline mode now works for notes and tasks, but attachments still need a connection, and we decided that is fine for the first release.

Now the two risks.
1) First, the App Store review has been slow this month, so we are submitting the build on Friday instead of next Wednesday to leave a buffer.
2) Second, the payments provider is changing their API on the fifteenth.
  Marcus is updating our integration, but if their sandbox is not ready we may have to launch with the old checkout flow and switch later.

For next week, design is finishing the onboarding screens and marketing is drafting the launch post.
If you have anything that should be in the release notes, send it to me by Thursday.
Thanks everyone, this has been a really solid week.
