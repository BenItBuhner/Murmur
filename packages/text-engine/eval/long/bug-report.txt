# bug-report | document
The desktop app sometimes pastes the dictation into the wrong window when you switch apps while it is still finishing.
It happens on Windows eleven and I could not reproduce it on the Mac.
It is not every time, but I can get it to happen about one in five tries.

Here is how to reproduce it.
1) Open a long document in Word and click somewhere in the middle of it.
2) Hold the dictation key and talk for at least a minute, so the release takes a few seconds.
3) Let go of the key and immediately switch to Slack with alt tab.
4) Wait for the dictation to finish.

What I expect is that the text goes into Word, because that is where I started dictating, or at least that the app tells me it did not paste.
What actually happens is that the text gets pasted into the Slack message box, and once it was sent to a channel before I noticed.
That last part is the scary one, because people saw a half finished thought that was meant for a document.

I looked at the logs, and the app reads the focused window at paste time instead of remembering the one from the start of the dictation.
I think the fix is to remember the window when recording starts and to only paste if that window is still in front.
If it is not, we should copy the text to the clipboard and show a notice instead.
This should be a high priority fix, since it can leak private text into a shared channel.
