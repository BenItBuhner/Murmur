# journal | notes
I have been thinking a lot about why the last month felt so slow even though I was busy every single day.
I think the honest answer is that I said yes to too many small things and never protected time for the one big thing.
Every morning started with messages, and by the time I got through them it was lunch and my energy was gone.

The days that did feel good had something in common.
I went for a walk before opening my laptop, I wrote for an hour before checking anything, and I had at most one meeting.
It is not complicated, but it only happened maybe four times in the whole month.
That is not a schedule problem, that is a decision I keep making badly in the moment.

So for November I want to try something simple.
The first two hours of the day belong to the application and the demo, and nothing else goes there unless it is truly urgent.
Messages wait until eleven.
If someone needs me earlier, they can call, and almost nobody will.

I also want to stop measuring the day by how many things I finished.
Some of the best days were the ones where I finished one thing that actually mattered.
I am going to write a short note every evening about the one thing I moved forward, and see how it looks after two weeks.
