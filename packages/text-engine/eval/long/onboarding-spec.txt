# onboarding-spec | document
This is the spec for the new onboarding flow, and the goal is to get someone from download to their first dictation in under two minutes.
Right now it takes most people closer to six minutes, and about a third of them never finish.
The biggest problem is that we ask for every permission up front before the person has seen anything work.

So the new flow has five steps.
1) Step one is a single welcome screen with one sentence about what the app does and a button that says get started.
2) Step two asks for microphone access, and only microphone access, with a short line explaining that audio is never stored without permission.
3) Step three is a practice dictation where the person reads a sentence out loud and sees it typed back to them.
  This is the moment where people understand the product, so it has to be fast and it has to look great.
4) Step four asks for accessibility access, because now the person knows why we need to type into other apps.
5) And finally, step five shows the keyboard shortcut and lets them change it before they leave.

We will measure two things.
The first is the share of people who finish onboarding, and we want that above eighty percent.
The second is how long it takes, and we want the median below two minutes.
We are going to run the old flow and the new flow side by side for two weeks before we switch everyone over.

Open questions are whether we need an account at all during onboarding, and whether the practice sentence should be in the person's own language.
My recommendation is no account until they have used the app for a day, and yes on the language.
