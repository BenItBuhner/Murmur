# meeting-notes | notes
Notes from the planning meeting on Monday with the platform team.
We spent most of the hour on the database migration, and the rest on hiring.

The migration is going ahead, but in stages instead of one big weekend.
We agreed on a few things.
- We move the read traffic first, because it is easy to roll back if the latency is worse.
- The write traffic moves only after a full week with no incidents on reads.
- Every stage gets a written rollback plan before it starts, and Dana owns those plans.
- We will not migrate the billing tables this quarter at all, since the risk is too high right before the holidays.
Nobody was fully happy with the timeline, but everyone agreed it is safer.

On hiring, we have two open roles, a senior backend engineer and a site reliability engineer.
The backend role has three candidates in the final round, and we expect an offer by the end of the month.
The reliability role has been open for ten weeks with almost no good applicants.
Sam suggested we widen the search to people who have done operations work at smaller companies, even without the exact title, and we all agreed to try that.

Action items are that Dana writes the first rollback plan, Sam updates the job posting, and I schedule the follow up for two weeks from now.
